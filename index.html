📜 Rules of Engagement

  • First quote: first correct answer earns 25 points and goes first.
  • Each round: 1 point for show/movie + 1 point for speaker.
  • Surrounding line: +1 for the line immediately before or after.
  • Defense: waiting player can hit I HAVE IT. Correct = +1; wrong = −1.
  • Captain's Chair Bonus: available after 5 or 10 rounds when mathematically possible.
  • Bonus: three choices. Correct answers = 10 points.
